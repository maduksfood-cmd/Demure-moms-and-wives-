Welcome to Demure

Demure Moms & Wives is a community created for women who desire meaningful connection, personal growth and intentional living while navigating the realities of motherhood and marriage.

We believe that a woman's life doesn't stop when she becomes a wife or mother.

There is still a woman with dreams.
A woman who wants friendship.
A woman who wants to build something.
A woman who wants to love her family deeply while also taking care of herself.
A woman who wants to learn, laugh, network, pray, grow and flourish.

That woman belongs here.

Our mission

To create a supportive and intentional sisterhood where moms and wives can connect, learn, grow and flourish through every season of life.

Our vision

To build a community where women feel seen, supported, empowered and connected, at home, in their relationships and in their personal lives.

More than motherhood. More than marriage.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Demure for?

Demure is primarily for women who are wives, mothers or navigating family life and want intentional connection, learning, friendship and personal growth.

Do I have to be married to join?

Demure is a community for moms and wives. If you are navigating motherhood, marriage or family life, you belong here.

How much is membership?

Membership is ₦2,000 per month.

What does my membership cover?

Your membership contributes toward community activities, member appreciation, birthdays, giveaways, selected expert sessions, resources and other Demure experiences.

Are all programmes free for members?

Not necessarily. Some special programmes may require an additional fee depending on the cost of bringing in a professional or organising the experience.

Is Demure only online?

No. Demure has both online experiences and physical gatherings, depending on the programme.

Can I invite a friend?

Absolutely. We love welcoming women into the sisterhood.
